Reform Life Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Reform Life is designed to help you store and organise health information that you enter. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified health professional.

1. Information stored

Depending on the features you use, the app may store information you enter, such as your profile details (for example your name, email address, date of birth, height, and weight, if you enter them), health readings, medicines, allergies, conditions, reminders, activity and wellness logs, notes, insurance policy details, and health-related documents or images. If you give permission, the app can also read supported activity information from Apple Health (described in section 3). The app also stores app settings and preferences, such as your goals, units, and whether you have confirmed that you are at least 16.

2. Where your information is stored

The current app stores its records locally on your iPhone. There is no online account: your profile exists only on your device. The app developer does not operate a server to receive your health records, and the app does not send your health records or personal information to the developer.

The app is configured to exclude health records, profile information, insurance details, photos, and documents from iCloud and computer backups. Some non-sensitive preferences and app-state information (for example goals, units, reminder times, and settings) may still be included in device backups. Backup and restore behaviour on real devices has not been independently verified, so do not rely on a backup either to include or to leave out this information.

Some features rely on services built into iOS, such as Apple Health, Face ID, text recognition for scanned documents, and notifications. These are provided by Apple under its own terms and privacy policy.

3. Health and device permissions

The app only asks for a permission when you use a feature that needs it, and you can change these permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

  • Apple Health: if you allow it, the app reads activity information such as steps, walking, running and cycling distance, active energy, exercise minutes, and workouts to show progress toward your goals. The app does not write any data to Apple Health.
  • Camera, photos, and files: used when you choose to take, scan, or import a photo, document, or file to keep with your records.
  • Motion: used to count steps and walking distance toward your goals.
  • Location: used only while the workout screen is open, when you turn on GPS for a workout you record, to measure distance. The app stores the workout distance, not your route.
  • Face ID or passcode: if you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your identity before showing protected sections. The app does not receive or store your face data.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Lock-screen reminder text is general and does not name your medicines, conditions, or measurements; a custom reminder's title is shown only if you choose to show it.

4. Sharing and exports

Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to export, copy, or share it. When you do, that information is handled by the app, person, or device you select, under their own privacy practices. Review the destination before sharing.

The health records export creates a CSV file containing your health readings only. It does not include documents, photos, insurance details, your profile, reminders, or wellness logs, and it is not a complete backup or a way to move all of your data to another phone. Documents can be shared individually.

To share or export a file, the app creates a temporary copy in its own storage on your iPhone. The app tries to remove these temporary copies shortly after sharing or exporting, and tries again at the next app launch, the next share or export, or when you use Delete all data. A temporary copy may sometimes remain for a while. Removal is ordinary file deletion and is not a secure erasure. Closing the share sheet does not mean the app or another person has finished using a file. Copies handed to other apps, people, or devices are outside the app's control and are not removed by the app.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. The app's Delete all data option removes your profile, records, documents, photos, insurance policies, reminders, and logs from this iPhone, together with temporary copies the app made for sharing or export. If a temporary copy cannot be removed, the app tells you before deleting anything and can try again at the next app launch. Deleting information is ordinary file deletion and is not a secure erasure.

Delete all data does not affect information in Apple Health, copies you previously exported or shared to other apps, people, or devices, or information included in existing device backups. Those copies may need to be deleted separately.

6. Device security and recovery

You are responsible for keeping your device and its passcode secure. Health records and profile information remain on the device, and the app is configured to exclude them from backups. They are not designed to transfer automatically to a new iPhone and may not be recoverable if your iPhone is lost, reset, or replaced. Backup and restore behaviour on real devices has not been independently verified. The CSV export covers health readings only, so keep your own copies of any documents or other information you need.

7. Analytics and advertising

The current version of the app does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

8. Subscriptions and payments

The current version of the app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and no paid features are available. If paid features are introduced, this policy will be updated before they launch to explain any related data handling. Apple processes App Store transactions under its own terms and privacy policy.

9. Minimum age

Reform Life is intended for people aged 16 and older. During setup, you are asked to confirm that you are at least 16 before you can continue. For this confirmation the app stores only a yes/no flag on your device; it does not ask for or store your date of birth or age for this purpose. The confirmation is a self-declaration and does not verify your actual age. If you are under 16, please do not use the app.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact us at support.reformlife@gmail.com.
