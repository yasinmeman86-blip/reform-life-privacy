Reform Life Privacy Policy

Draft — publication pending verification

Reform Life is designed to help you store and organise health information that you enter. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

1. Information stored

Depending on the features you use, the app may store information you enter, such as your profile details, health readings, medicines, allergies, reminders, activity logs, notes, and health-related documents or images. Information available to the app may also include data you choose to import or access through Apple Health and device features.

2. Where your information is stored

The current app is designed to store its records locally on your iPhone. The app developer does not operate a server to receive your health records. The app's current backup settings are intended to exclude health records, profile information, photos, and documents from iPhone backups. Some non-sensitive preferences and app-state information may still be included in device backups.

3. Health and device permissions

If you grant permission, the app may access supported Apple Health information or device features needed for functions you choose to use. Camera, photo, document scanning, file import, motion, or location access may be used for the relevant feature. Location information used during a workout may be processed on the device; the app's current design stores workout distance rather than a route history.

4. Sharing and exports

The app does not send your health records to the developer's server. When you choose to export, copy, or share information, that information may leave the app and be handled by the destination you select. Review the destination and its privacy practices before sharing.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app's data. The app provides a Delete Everything option for data covered by that feature. Copies you previously exported, shared, or included in older device backups may remain outside the app and may need to be deleted separately. Existing backups are not automatically erased by deleting information from the app.

6. Device security and recovery

You are responsible for keeping your device and its passcode secure. Because health records and profile information are intended to remain on the device and be excluded from backups, they may not be recoverable if your iPhone is lost, reset, or replaced. Export important information you need to keep.

7. Analytics and advertising

The current app audit found no analytics or advertising SDKs. This statement must be reviewed if analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other third-party services are added in a future version.

8. Subscriptions and payments

Paid subscription features are planned but are not yet implemented in the current app version. If subscriptions are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain relevant payment processing, subscription status, and related data handling before those features launch. Apple may process transactions under its own terms and privacy policy.

9. Children's privacy

Reform Life is not intended to be used by children without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. Parents or guardians should manage any health information entered for a child and review applicable local requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact us at support.reformlife@gmail.com.
